**Database Systems Design Class**

_Languages: PHP, MySQL_

Traffic See is a platform dedicated to providing real-time information about traffic incidents and events to 
help commuters plan their routes efficiently. Our goal is to enhance the commuting experience by delivering 
timely and accurate updates on road conditions, construction, accidents, and other incidents that may affect travel.

Our mission is to empower commuters with the information they need to make informed decisions about their daily travels 
and information about the events. By offering a comprehensive view of traffic incidents, we aim to reduce commute times, 
minimize disruptions, and contribute to a smoother and safer transportation experience.
